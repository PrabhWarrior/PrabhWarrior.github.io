About me

I like the parts of a system nobody sees until they break.

Most of my work sits below the API: queues, consumers, storage and the failure cases in between. I can build the React side too, but the backend is where I do my best work.

How I build

Designing for the bad day, not the demo

  1. Fault tolerant by default

    Outbox, ordered commits and dedup, tested against crashes and rebalances.

  2. Scale by partitioning

    Partition-owned work and lag-based autoscaling, no shared locks.

  3. Robust under pressure

    Backpressure, retries and dead-letter queues, so a slow dependency stays contained.

  4. Measure honestly

    Benchmarks under production settings, not developer defaults.
